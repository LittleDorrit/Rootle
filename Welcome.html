🔗 Get Started

Click here to download the test version of the Rootle app from Google Play.

Important:
This version is part of Google’s Closed Testing Track, so:
  • You must have a Gmail account
  • You must be on an Android device
  • Sign in with your personal Gmail account when prompted

🛠️ What to Expect During Testing

  • The app will walk you through a subscription process, but you will not have to enter any payment info
  • The subscription may expire or close every 15–20 minutes (this is normal during testing) -- but you only have to use it for a few minutes a day

✅ Participation Requirements

  1. Use the app for at least a few minutes every day for 14 days
  2. Optional: Complete a 3–5 minute feedback form at the end of testing
  3. Contact us if you encounter any issues

🎁 Reward Info

  • You'll receive a $10 Amazon gift card via email after completing the 14-day test period
  • Limited to the first 12 testers, so please get started right away!
