4. Account Deletion Instructions

You may request deletion of your Rootle account and all associated data at any time. We offer multiple ways to initiate this process depending on your platform:

From Within the App (Android & iOS)

If you are signed in to the Rootle app:

  • Go to Settings
  • Tap Delete Account
  • Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your request

This option is available on both Android and iOS.

From a Web Browser (All Users)

If you no longer have the app installed or prefer to submit your request online:

  • Visit our Account Deletion Request page:
    https://littledorrit.github.io/Rootle/delete_account.html
  • Enter the email address associated with your Rootle account
  • Submit the form
  • We will send a verification code to confirm your identity and proceed with deletion

What Happens After Deletion

Once your account is deleted:

  • Your email address and any usage data associated with your account will be removed from our authentication system and backend database
  • Your subscription status will no longer be linked to any user ID
  • Your access to all Rootle features will be revoked

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

We may retain anonymized or aggregated data for analytics purposes as permitted by law.
