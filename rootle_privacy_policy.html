Rootle Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2025

Last Updated: September 10, 2025

Rootle ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Rootle mobile application (the "App"), which is designed to help users build biomedical English vocabulary through interactive learning.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect limited personally identifiable information when you create an account, such as your email address and, optionally, your name. This allows you to log in across devices and securely access your personalized account.

To improve our services and provide core functionality, we may also collect the following information automatically:

  • App usage data (screens visited, time spent)
  • Crash logs and error reports
  • Subscription status and transaction history
  • Anonymous identifiers (such as device ID or Firebase Instance ID)

2. Data Collected via Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party tools to provide functionality and analytics:

Firebase (Google LLC)

Used for:

  • Authentication (login and account creation via email)
  • Real-time database access (content delivery)
  • Crash reporting and error logs
  • Analytics (anonymized)
  • Cloud storage (audio files)

Firebase may collect device type, OS version, country, and crash stack traces. Learn more.

Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchases

Used for processing subscription payments. We do not see or store your credit card or personal billing information — this is handled securely by Google or Apple.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used solely for:

  • Authenticating your login
  • Syncing your progress and preferences across devices
  • Sending password reset or verification emails

We do not use your email address for marketing purposes, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

We use other collected data to:

  • Improve app functionality and performance
  • Understand how users engage with the content
  • Detect and fix bugs
  • Enable optional premium features (e.g., subscription access)

4. Account Deletion

To delete your account, open the app and go to Settings > Delete account. Your email address and other saved settings will be removed from our database permanently.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your email address and any usage data associated with your account will be removed from our authentication system and backend database
  • Your subscription status will no longer be linked to any user ID
  • Your access to all Rootle features will be revoked
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone

We may retain anonymized or aggregated data for analytics purposes as permitted by law.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain non-personal data as long as necessary to improve our services. All data is stored on secure, encrypted servers provided by Firebase. We do not sell or rent your data to any third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Rootle is intended for students 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us.

7. Your Rights and Controls

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no account or profile to delete. However, if you have questions about your data or wish to report a concern, you may contact us (see below).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Effective Date.” Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any changes.
